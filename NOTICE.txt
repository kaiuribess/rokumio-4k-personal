This personal fork adds 4K-first direct HTTPS stream selection, 1080p choices,
voice search/playback wiring, native playback diagnostics, and addon protocol
fixes, English audio recovery, cleaner source cards, projector/surround presets,
source preloading, automatic next-episode playback, a red interface, add-on
checks, equipment setup guidance, bounded cinematic Home previews, saved
playback preferences, timer/position stability fixes, early automatic source
selection, a shared source-request budget, bounded source pages and opaque
preferred/working-provider scheduling. It is not an official
Roku, Stremio, TorBox, or Hisense product.

No movies, series, subscription accounts, or stream-provider credentials are
bundled. Cinemeta provides metadata; installed providers supply playable URLs.
